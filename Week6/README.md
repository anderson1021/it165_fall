# Week 6 — CSS Transitions, Animations & JavaScript
Student: Anderson Benitez Ortiz

## Open the project
Extract the ZIP, open the Week6 folder, and double-click index.html. No installation or internet connection is needed. Keep the folder structure intact.

## Files
- index.html: the page, cards, animation frame, and slideshow controls.
- style.css: layout, 0.3-second transitions, translateY(-8px), scale(1.02), elevation shadows, and the 0%/50%/100% rotation and border-color animation.
- script.js: five image-path strings, a currentIndex variable, getElementById references, and previous/next wrap-around logic.
- images/: five included SVG landscape illustrations. SVG files are images supported directly by browsers.

## Understand and check your work
1. Hover over a card. It moves upward 8px, grows by 2%, and gains a stronger shadow over 0.3 seconds.
2. Watch the frame rotate and change border color. At 0% it is teal/0 degrees; at 50% orange/180 degrees; at 100% teal/360 degrees.
3. Click Next through all five images. After image 5, image 1 should appear.
4. From image 1, click Previous. Image 5 should appear.
5. The human-readable counter is currentIndex + 1; JavaScript array indexes remain 0–4.
6. If your computer uses reduced motion, animation and hover movement are intentionally disabled for accessibility.

Review and personalize the example so you can explain every part of your submission.

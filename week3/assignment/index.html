About Me

Hello! I am Anderson, an IT student in New York City. I enjoy learning how computers and networks work, and I am building my skills through hands-on assignments. I also have several years of experience working at Equinox, where I have learned to be dependable, organized, and helpful to others.

My Favorite Activity: Building Computer Networks

I like practicing network setup in Cisco Packet Tracer. I can connect computers, switches, and routers in a virtual workspace and see how information travels between them. My favorite part is troubleshooting: when two devices cannot communicate, I check the cables, IP addresses, gateways, and switch settings until I find the issue. It feels rewarding when a successful ping shows that the network works.

What I practice

  1. Plan a network and assign IP addresses to devices.
  2. Connect computers, switches, and routers.
  3. Configure settings such as gateways and VLANs.
  4. Test connectivity and troubleshoot problems.
